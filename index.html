Math Chain Privacy Policy

Android and iPhone application com.zengothic.mathchain · Effective 10 October 2026

In one paragraph: Math Chain has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of ours. The only thing we receive is a crash report when something goes wrong in the game — it contains no personal details, and we use it solely to fix bugs. Your progress is saved only on your own device. The game is free and shows advertisements, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app collects and shares device information — including your Android advertising ID, or on iPhone Apple's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking — in order to select, show and measure those ads. Optional in-app purchases are paid through the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details.

1. Who this policy is from

Math Chain is published by Zengothic, an independent developer. This policy applies to the Math Chain application for Android and iPhone (identifier com.zengothic.mathchain) and to nothing else. There is no Math Chain website, web version or online service.

2. Information we collect

Only crash reports. Math Chain has no registration, no login, no profile, no chat, no leaderboard, no cloud save and no analytics. We operate no servers of our own. The one kind of information that reaches us is a crash report, described below.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, or an error breaks part of the game, a crash report is sent to us through Google's Firebase Crashlytics so that we can find and fix the problem. A report contains:

  • the error message and the technical trace of where in the app's code it happened
  • where you were in the game at the time — for example the level number — and the last few steps you took in the app
  • device and app details: phone model, Android version, app version, language, and technical state such as free memory and screen orientation
  • a random identifier that Crashlytics creates for this installation of the app, so that repeated reports can be counted; it changes if you reinstall the app

A crash report does not contain your name, email address, account, advertising ID, location or anything you have typed, and we never attach any identifier of our own. We use crash reports only to fix bugs. Google processes them on our behalf as a service provider under the Firebase terms, keeps them for 90 days and then deletes them automatically. Google Analytics is switched off for the app.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps your progress in the application's own private storage so you can continue where you left off. It leaves your device only through Android's own two copying mechanisms, both of which you control in your device settings: cloud backup to your Google account, and device-to-device transfer when you set up a new phone. The game permits both so that changing handset does not cost you your progress, and it permits them for the saved game and nothing else. It consists of:

  • which levels you have finished and the stars you earned
  • your in-game coin balance and how far you have read the in-game story
  • how many hints you have used, and the daily puzzle you have played
  • your sound, music and vibration settings

None of this identifies you. It contains no name, no email address and no account identifier, because the game never asks for any.

3. Information we do not collect

To be explicit, Math Chain does not collect, request or use any of the following:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account credentials
  • precise or coarse location from the device's location services — the app holds no location permission of any kind
  • camera, microphone, photos, files, contacts, calendar or call/SMS data — none of these permissions is requested
  • health, financial or payment information — optional in-app purchases (coin packs, Remove Ads) are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play; the game receives only a confirmation of what was bought, never your card or account details
  • anything you type or create — there is no chat, no username, no comments and no user-generated content anywhere in the game
  • push notifications — no notification service is integrated
  • analytics of how you play — no analytics service is configured, and Google Analytics is switched off

The app asks for no permission to anything on your device or about you — nothing that would let it read your location, your files, your camera, your microphone, your contacts or your messages. The complete permission list in the installed app is short, and every entry is either infrastructure or required by the advertising SDK:

  • INTERNET — declared by us, used to request advertisements and to send the crash reports described in section 2.
  • VIBRATE — declared by us, for the optional haptic feedback you can turn off in Settings.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS — added automatically by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so that it can request and measure the ads described in section 5.
  • com.zengothic.mathchain.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION — this one is not an Android permission at all. It is a permission the app defines for itself, added automatically by Android's own AndroidX libraries for apps that target Android 13 or later, and it is declared at signature protection level. It grants nothing to anything except code signed with our own key, requests no device capability and no information about you, and it exists to make the app more locked down, not less: it stops other apps on your phone from reaching the app's internal broadcast receivers.

None of these produces a permission prompt, because none of them is the kind of permission Android asks you to approve.

4. How information is used

  • By us: crash reports only, used solely to find and fix bugs in the game.
  • On your device: to remember your progress and settings between sessions.
  • By Google: to select, deliver, cap the frequency of, and measure advertisements, and to detect invalid activity and fraud.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

The game is free and is funded by advertising. Two formats are used:

  • Interstitial ads — an occasional full-screen ad between levels. They are paced, are never shown to a new player, and never appear after a level you have just lost.
  • Rewarded ads — always optional and always your choice. You may watch one to receive an extra hint or to double the coins from a level. The game is completely playable without ever watching one.

There is no banner ad and no App Open ad format in the app, and no advertising network other than Google is integrated.

Google's handling of the information described above is covered by:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Personalised and non-personalised advertising

Whether the ads you see are personalised depends on your own device settings and, in some regions, on the consent choice described below. If personalised advertising is switched off, or if consent is declined where consent is required, you will still see advertisements — they will simply be non-personalised, which means they are selected without using your advertising ID to build interest categories. Google does not serve personalised advertising to users it knows to be under 18.

6. Consent and your privacy choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

Math Chain uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for consent where the law requires it. When a consent message applies to your region, it is presented when the app starts, and your choice is passed to Google and applied to the advertising described above.

You can change that choice at any time without reinstalling. Open the game's Settings and tap PRIVACY OPTIONS → MANAGE to re-open the consent message. The row appears only when Google's consent framework reports that a privacy-options form applies to you, so if your region has no applicable message you will not see it.

On any device, at any time

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID — Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by manufacturer). Deleting the ID stops apps from receiving it at all.
  • Turn off ad personalisation — the same Android settings screen.
  • On iPhone — Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking lets you turn tracking off for Math Chain (or all apps) at any time.
  • Remove the saved game from your device — clear the app's storage, or uninstall it.
  • Remove the backup copy too — if Android backup is switched on, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game does not delete the copy already held in your Google account. Delete it in the Google One app or at one.google.com → Storage → Backups, and turn future copies off under Settings → Google → Backup.

8. Children's privacy

Math Chain is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and it is not enrolled in Google Play's Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and the app collects no personal information from anyone; crash reports contain none. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at the address in section 1.

9. Data retention and deletion

The only data we hold is crash reports, which Firebase Crashlytics deletes automatically 90 days after they are received. Because a report carries no name, email or account, we cannot pick out the reports of a particular person to export or delete them earlier. The saved game on your device is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. If Android backup is switched on, a copy of that saved game also sits in your Google account, and it is not deleted by uninstalling — section 6 explains how to delete it and how to stop further copies being made. For information Google holds in connection with advertising, use the Android advertising-ID controls in section 6 together with Google My Ad Center and Google's own privacy tools.

10. Data security

Game data is stored inside the application's private sandbox on your device and is protected by Android's application isolation. The only information sent to us is crash reports, which are stored by Google's Firebase under Google's security controls and contain no personal details. Crash reports and the information sent to Google by the ads SDK are transmitted over encrypted connections.

11. International transfers

Crash reports, and information collected by Google through the advertising SDK, may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under Google's own safeguards and privacy policy.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information, or to object to it. Math Chain sends us nothing but crash reports, which contain no name, email or account and so cannot be matched to you; they are deleted automatically after 90 days. The practical controls available to you are those in section 6, and any request concerning information held by Google should be directed to Google. If you are unsure, write to us at the address in section 1 and we will help as far as we are able.

This policy describes what the app does. It is not a claim of compliance with the law of every jurisdiction.
